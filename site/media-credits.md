## Screenshots and GIFs

Actual Slay the Spire 2 game-window captures of The Architect's Trial from local
development sessions on September 16, 2026. Windows title bars were cropped;
the game interface and artwork were retained. Optimized copies are WebP.

The three GIFs are timed sequences of existing screenshots, **not recordings of
continuous gameplay**. They do not imply a naturally completed run. Card/review
fixtures can show earlier presentation states, an explicit acceptance button,
the QA-only review toolbar and View run button. Current proposals apply
automatically after an eight-second override window, as described on the page.
Normal play does not show the QA controls.

The exact source paths and ordered GIF frames are in
[assets/media-manifest.json](assets/media-manifest.json). Source captures live
under the repository's ignored artifacts directory. Existing optimized website
assets are checked in, so publishing requires none of those private files.

## Potion illustrations

Eight original potion illustrations created for this mod with ImageGen on
September 16, 2026. Source paintings and generation notes are in the main
repository's assets/potions directory. The site uses optimized transparent WebP
copies. The internal brittle_brew filename is displayed as Fracture Flask.
