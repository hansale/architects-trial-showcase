# The Architect's potion rack

One potion per ten-second window before the Champion's turn. Four public slots.

| Potion | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Weakening Brew | The Champion gains 2 Weak. |
| Fracture Flask | The Champion gains 2 Vulnerable. |
| Updraft Tonic | Living enemies gain 5 Flight. |
| Frailty Flask | The Champion gains 1 Frail. |
| Sludge Bottle | Add 2 temporary Dazed to the Champion's combat discard pile. |
| Bottled Spite | Shuffle 2 random Curses into the Champion's draw pile for this combat. |
| Timeworn Tonic | Living enemies gain 1 Strength every 12 cards played. The turn continues, and reapplication preserves counters. |
| Fogbound Flask | The next normal hand draw is reduced by 5, to a minimum of 0; other draw effects are unaffected. |

Chest openings and overrides award a choice of three potions. At capacity,
replace one stored potion or discard the incoming potion.
